**What it is**
Apply your organization's brand kit, logo, colors, fonts, and visual style, to images and other visuals generated in Copilot chat and Create.[^1]

**Why it matters**
A generic AI-generated visual usually needs manual rework before anyone can use it on a real deliverable. Brand Kit removes that step by feeding the org's existing style rules into generation, so the output is closer to publish-ready the first time.

**Who it's for**
Comms, marketing, and anyone generating client-facing or org-facing visuals through Copilot.

**How to access**
Applied automatically when generating visuals in Copilot chat, once an admin has configured a brand kit for the org.

**Requires**
Org-level brand kit configuration, set by an admin.
