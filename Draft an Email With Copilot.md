**What it is** — Copilot drafts a new email or reply from a prompt, with tone and length options.

**Why it matters** — cuts the time spent staring at a blank compose window, while tone/length controls keep the output adjustable rather than one-size-fits-all.

**Who it's for** — anyone who drafts email regularly, especially routine or templated correspondence.

**How to access** — draft option in Outlook's message compose window.

**Requires** — Copilot add-on license. Compose window must be in HTML format — Copilot drafting isn't available in plain-text compose.
