**What it is**
Copilot builds charts, PivotTables, and shapes from a request instead of requiring manual configuration.

**Why it matters**
Raw rows become a shareable visual in one step instead of several menus.

**Who it's for**
Anyone preparing data for a review meeting or a deck.

**How to access**
Copilot pane in Excel.

**Requires**
Microsoft 365 Copilot (add-on) license. Not included in Copilot Chat (Basic).

## Try it
1. Open the Copilot pane in Excel.
2. Describe the chart or PivotTable you want.

> Example prompt: Build a PivotTable summarizing revenue by product and quarter
