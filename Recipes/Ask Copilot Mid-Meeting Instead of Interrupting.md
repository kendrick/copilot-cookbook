**The ask**
Catching up or checking a fact shouldn't mean interrupting the meeting or DMing a colleague. Ask Copilot privately, against the live transcript, and stay in the flow.

**What you'll use**
- [Copilot During a Meeting](../Surfaces/Teams/Copilot%20During%20a%20Meeting.md) — real-time assistance grounded in the meeting transcript as it's generated

**Steps**
1. Open the Copilot panel inside the active meeting.
2. Ask what you missed or what's been decided so far.
3. Keep it to yourself and keep the meeting moving.

**Prompts**
```
What have I missed so far, and what's been decided?
```
```
What questions have been raised that nobody has answered yet?
```

**Watch out for**
Needs the add-on and a live transcript, so transcription has to be turned on. Copilot reads the transcript, not the room, tone, hesitation, and the thing nobody said out loud aren't in scope. Your questions are private to you.
