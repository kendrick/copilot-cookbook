**The ask**
A draft lands in your lap that's most of the way there and reads like it. You need to understand it fast, then tighten, restructure, and fix the tone in place, not rebuild it from a blank page.

**What you'll use**
- [Chat With Your Document in Word](../Surfaces/Word/Chat%20With%20Your%20Document%20in%20Word.md) — answers questions and finds the weak spots without a full reread
- [Rewrite and Refine in Word](../Surfaces/Word/Rewrite%20and%20Refine%20in%20Word.md) — rewrites selected passages in place
- [Copilot Edits Your Document](../Surfaces/Word/Copilot%20Edits%20Your%20Document.md) — adds the missing pieces directly into the document

**Steps**
1. Ask the chat pane what the document says and where it's unclear, redundant, or unsupported.
2. Select the rough sections and rewrite them in place.
3. Use Copilot Edits to add what's missing, an executive summary, a conclusion.
4. Do a final tone pass so it reads as one voice, not two.

**Prompts**
```
Summarize this document and flag any sections that are unclear, redundant, or
make a claim without support.
```
```
Rewrite this section to be more concise and direct, same meaning.
```
```
Add a short executive summary at the top, based on the document's content.
```

**Watch out for**
All three need the add-on. Copilot now edits in place by default, so review each change before you accept it; it stays confident even when it's wrong about a fact only you can check. Fixing the words doesn't fix a wrong premise underneath them.
