**The ask**
The conversations that matter most are the ones you least want to wing. Rehearse the approach and get feedback before you're in the room, so you're not workshopping your tone while someone's face falls.

**What you'll use**
- [Coaching Agents](../Surfaces/Agents%20%26%20Agent%20Builder/Coaching%20Agents.md) — the Career and communication coaches critique your plan; the Learning agent's practice simulations let you rehearse the conversation itself

**Steps**
1. Describe the situation and who you're talking to, and lay out your planned approach.
2. Ask a coaching agent to critique the approach and suggest a stronger opening.
3. For interactive practice, use the Learning agent's role-play simulation and get feedback on how it landed.
4. Adjust, and go in having already said the hard part once.

**Prompts**
```
I need to tell a report their project is being cut. Here's my plan for the
conversation: [paste]. Critique my approach and suggest a better opening line.
```

**Watch out for**
The coaching agents ship inside Copilot Chat at no add-on cost. The interactive role-play runs through the Learning agent's practice simulations (built on Skillsoft's CAISY), which may require Viva Learning in your org, so check availability before you promise a team it's there. It's rehearsal, not a script; the real conversation still needs you actually present in it.
