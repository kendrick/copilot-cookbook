**The ask**
A reorg means the product marketing role and the sales role are becoming one job, and you need a posting that keeps the strongest requirements from each without reading like two documents stapled together.

**What you'll use**
- [Draft with Copilot in Word](../Surfaces/Word/Draft%20with%20Copilot%20in%20Word.md) with [Ground a Draft on Your Files](../Surfaces/Word/Ground%20a%20Draft%20on%20Your%20Files.md) — blends both source documents into a first draft
- [Copilot Edits Your Document](../Surfaces/Word/Copilot%20Edits%20Your%20Document.md) — merges duplicate sections and reorders what's left
- [Rewrite and Refine in Word](../Surfaces/Word/Rewrite%20and%20Refine%20in%20Word.md) — a tone pass once the structure holds

**Steps**
1. Attach both job descriptions in Word.
2. Ask Copilot to blend them into a single posting, grounded on both files.
3. Use Copilot Edits Your Document to merge duplicate sections and fix ordering.
4. Run a rewrite pass for tone and consistency before it goes out.

**Prompts**
```
Generate a job description that blends a product marketing strategist and a
sales role based on these two job descriptions. Keep the strongest
requirements from each and remove duplicates.
```

**Watch out for**
Ground a Draft on Your Files needs both source documents in SharePoint or OneDrive. Copilot Edits Your Document is new as of 2026-06, so check whether it's still rolling out in your tenant before you rely on it for a live posting.
