**The ask**
Most of the time Auto is fine and you never think about it. But when a hard question gets a thin answer, the fix is often the mode or the model, not the wording, and it's worth knowing which lever to pull.

**What you'll use**
- [Choose Your AI Model](../Surfaces/Copilot%20Chat/Choose%20Your%20AI%20Model.md) — Auto, Quick response, and Think Deeper modes
- [Use Claude and GPT-5.5 in Copilot](../Surfaces/Copilot%20Chat/Use%20Claude%20and%20GPT-5.5%20in%20Copilot.md) — frontier models from Anthropic and OpenAI, if your admin has enabled them

**Steps**
1. Leave it on Auto for everyday questions.
2. When a hard question comes back shallow, switch to Think Deeper and ask again.
3. If your admin has enabled Claude or GPT-5.5, try one on reasoning- or writing-heavy work and compare.

**Prompts**
```
Think through the trade-offs of moving our support team to a follow-the-sun
model. Argue both sides before you recommend one.
```

**Watch out for**
The modes are available on both tiers, but the frontier models are add-on plus an admin opt-in, set per user or group. So what you can actually select depends on what your admin switched on, not just your license, worth checking before you tell a colleague to "just use Claude" and they don't have it.
