**The ask**
A multilingual meeting shouldn't run at the speed of whoever's translating in their head. Let everyone hear it live in their own language, no human interpreter, no translated recap after the fact.

**What you'll use**
- [Interpreter in Meetings](../Surfaces/Teams/Interpreter%20in%20Meetings.md) — real-time speech-to-speech interpretation, in a voice that echoes the speaker's, for meetings up to 1,000 people

**Steps**
1. Confirm the organizer holds a Copilot or Teams Premium license before the meeting.
2. In the meeting, each person opens More actions > Language and speech > Interpreter and turns it on.
3. Each participant picks the language they want to hear.
4. Run the meeting normally, everyone hears it in their own language.

**Watch out for**
Interpreter is GA and needs the Microsoft 365 Copilot license, with 20 hours per person per month included; the organizer needs Copilot or Teams Premium. It supports a fixed set of languages and isn't available in ad-hoc, Teams Free, town hall, or webinar meetings. It interprets speech in real time, so idioms and fine nuance can still slip, confirm anything critical directly.
