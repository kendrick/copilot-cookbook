**The ask**
Not everyone needs the paid add-on, and not everyone who wants it will use it. Match roles to the right tier, and let readiness signals point the paid seats where they'll do the most.

**What you'll use**
- [Copilot Licensing Tiers](../Surfaces/Admin%20%26%20Governance/Copilot%20Licensing%20Tiers.md) — the three tiers and what each one actually unlocks
- [Copilot Adoption Analytics](../Surfaces/Admin%20%26%20Governance/Copilot%20Adoption%20Analytics.md) — the Copilot Dashboard's Readiness view and its recommendations on where to focus

**Steps**
1. Map roles to tiers: who genuinely needs work-grounded, in-app Copilot everywhere (the add-on) versus who's well served by free Basic chat.
2. Check the dashboard's Readiness view for where activation and early usage suggest the next seats should go.
3. Assign the seats through the Copilot Control System.
4. Revisit as the dashboard shows who's actually taking it up.

**Watch out for**
All of this lives in the admin and governance surfaces, so it's a champion-and-sponsor recipe more than an end-user one. The Readiness view is a recommendation, not a verdict, weigh it against what you know about the roles. And "who should get a seat" is a different question from "did the seats pay off," which the companion recipe below handles.
