**The ask**
The deck exists, it's just wordy, missing a couple of slides, and dressed in clip art. Fix what's there inside PowerPoint instead of starting over.

**What you'll use**
- [Rewrite and Summarize Slides](../Surfaces/PowerPoint/Rewrite%20and%20Summarize%20Slides.md) — tightens slide text and can summarize the whole deck
- [Add and Organize Slides](../Surfaces/PowerPoint/Add%20and%20Organize%20Slides.md) — adds the slides the story is missing
- [Generate Images in PowerPoint](../Surfaces/PowerPoint/Generate%20Images%20in%20PowerPoint.md) — replaces stock or placeholder art with original images

**Steps**
1. Ask Copilot to summarize the deck and flag the slides that are too wordy or off-message.
2. Rewrite the worst offenders.
3. Add the slide or two the narrative is missing.
4. Generate original images where the visuals are letting it down.

**Prompts**
```
Summarize this deck and tell me which slides are too wordy or don't fit the
main message.
```
```
Add a slide on competitive differentiation that fits the existing flow.
```
```
Generate an image for the title slide showing a team shipping a product.
```

**Watch out for**
All three need the add-on. Copilot rewrites and adds against what's already in the deck, so a slide built on a wrong premise comes back polished but still wrong, fix the underlying point, not just the wording.
