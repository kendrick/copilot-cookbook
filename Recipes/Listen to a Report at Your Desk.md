**The ask**
A dense document lands and you need the shape of it before you can read the parts that matter. Listen to an overview while you do something else, then ask the document the specific questions the overview raised.

**What you'll use**
- [Audio Overview in Word](../Surfaces/Word/Audio%20Overview%20in%20Word.md) — a spoken overview of the document you can listen to
- [Chat With Your Document in Word](../Surfaces/Word/Chat%20With%20Your%20Document%20in%20Word.md) — a chat pane that answers and cites from the open document

**Steps**
1. Open the document in Word for the web or Word for Windows and generate the audio overview.
2. Listen for the gist and note where you need detail.
3. Open the Copilot chat pane and ask the pointed questions, letting it cite the source passages.

**Prompts**
```
What are the key decisions this document is asking me to make, and where in
the document is each one?
```

**Watch out for**
Audio Overview runs in Word for the web and Word for Windows; the Word mobile app doesn't generate it, so this is a desk or laptop move, not a commute one. Both features need the add-on. The overview is a first pass, not a substitute for reading the sections you're on the hook for.
