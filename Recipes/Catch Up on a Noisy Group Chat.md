**The ask**
A busy Teams chat moves fast, and scrolling back to find the two things that actually need you is a waste of ten minutes. Ask Copilot what you missed in that one thread and what's waiting on you.

**What you'll use**
- [Copilot in Teams Chat](../Surfaces/Teams/Copilot%20in%20Teams%20Chat.md) — summarizes a single chat thread, cited, with up to 30 days of lookback

**Steps**
1. Open the chat and start Copilot inside it.
2. Ask for a summary of what you missed and, specifically, anything directed at you.
3. Follow up on the one or two threads that need a reply or a decision.

**Prompts**
```
Summarize what I missed in this chat since this morning. Call out anything
that needs a decision or a reply from me.
```
```
What did Priya say about the launch date?
```

**Watch out for**
This is scoped to one chat thread and looks back up to 30 days, so it's the same-day-triage tool, not the after-vacation tool. Full functionality needs the add-on; Basic gives you a lighter version. When you're rebuilding context across every app after real time away, that's a different job, see below.
