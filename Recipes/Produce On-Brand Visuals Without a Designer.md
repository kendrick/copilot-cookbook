**The ask**
You need a usable visual now, and you'd like it to look like it came from your company rather than from a generic image generator, without opening a design tool to get there.

**What you'll use**
- [Generate Images in Copilot Chat](../Surfaces/Copilot%20Chat/Generate%20Images%20in%20Copilot%20Chat.md) — a quick raw image, on any tier
- [Create in the Copilot App](../Surfaces/Create/Create%20in%20the%20Copilot%20App.md) — the fuller content studio for images, posters, and more
- [Brand Kit for Visuals](../Surfaces/Create/Brand%20Kit%20for%20Visuals.md) — applies the org's logo, colors, and fonts to what you generate

**Steps**
1. Describe the image and generate a first pass in Copilot Chat.
2. For a more finished asset, a poster, a header, move into Create.
3. If your org has a Brand Kit configured, generation applies it, so the output lands closer to publish-ready.

**Prompts**
```
Generate a clean header image for a post about our summer internship program,
warm and optimistic, no text.
```
```
Make a poster version of this with room for a headline across the top.
```

**Watch out for**
The tier fork matters here: quick image generation in Copilot Chat sits on the free Basic tier, but Brand Kit is a paid, admin-configured capability, so whether your output comes out on-brand depends on your license and on an admin having set the kit up. Either way, generated art is a starting point, so look at it closely before it goes on anything client-facing.
