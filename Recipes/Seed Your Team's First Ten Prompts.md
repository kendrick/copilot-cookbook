**The ask**
The blank Copilot box is where adoption goes to die. Hand people a short, role-relevant starter set they can run today, plus a coach for when they're ready to write their own.

**What you'll use**
- [Prompt Gallery](../Surfaces/Copilot%20Chat/Prompt%20Gallery.md) — browse, save, and share ready-made prompts by app and role
- [Coaching Agents](../Surfaces/Agents%20%26%20Agent%20Builder/Coaching%20Agents.md) — the Prompt Coach helps people structure a better prompt

**Steps**
1. Pull ten prompts from the gallery that match your team's actual work, not a generic set.
2. Save and share them so everyone starts from the same place.
3. Point people at the Prompt Coach for when they outgrow the starter prompts.
4. Check back on which ones stuck, and swap out the ones that didn't.

**Prompts**
```
Summarize this email thread and draft a reply in my voice, keeping it short.
```
```
Help me write a prompt that turns my rough meeting notes into a status update
for leadership.
```

**Watch out for**
The gallery and the coaching agents are free on Basic, so this works for the whole team, not just add-on seats. Aim for ten prompts people actually use, not a library of a hundred nobody opens, keep the starter set short and specific to what the team does.
