**The ask**
Sometimes typing a description of the problem takes longer than the problem does. Show Copilot your screen or point your camera at it and ask about what it sees.

**What you'll use**
- [Vision in Copilot](../Surfaces/Copilot%20Chat/Vision%20in%20Copilot.md) — shares your desktop screen (Windows or web) or your mobile camera during a voice conversation

**Steps**
1. Start a voice conversation in Copilot.
2. Share your desktop screen, or point your phone's camera at what you're looking at.
3. Ask about what's in view.

**Prompts**
```
What is this error on my screen telling me, and what should I try first?
```
```
Turn what's on this whiteboard into a structured list of next steps.
```

**Watch out for**
Vision is still rolling out (since June 2026), so it may not have reached your tenant yet. It works on the desktop screen and the mobile camera, in a voice conversation. Treat what you show it like any other Copilot input, don't point it at something you wouldn't otherwise put into a prompt.
