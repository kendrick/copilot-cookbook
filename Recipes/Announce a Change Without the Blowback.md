**The ask**
An org-wide announcement lands differently than a one-to-one email: everyone reads it, and the comments are public. Draft it from the actual decision, refine the tone for a broad audience, and post it where the org already gathers.

**What you'll use**
- [Work-Grounded Copilot Chat](../Surfaces/Copilot%20Chat/Work-Grounded%20Copilot%20Chat.md) — drafts the announcement from the real source: the decision memo, the FAQ, the thread where it was settled
- [Copilot in Viva Engage](../Surfaces/Viva%20Engage/Copilot%20in%20Viva%20Engage.md) — composes and refines the post or storyline in place, then publishes to the community

**Steps**
1. Ask Chat to draft the announcement from the source decision, not from scratch.
2. Move into Viva Engage and refine the tone for an all-employee, mixed audience.
3. Post to the right community or storyline.
4. Watch the early replies and follow up where the questions cluster.

**Prompts**
```
Draft a company-wide announcement about the office move, based on
/decision-memo.docx. Plain and warm, specific about what changes and when.
```
```
Rewrite this for an all-employee audience: less jargon, acknowledge the
disruption honestly, and end with where people can ask questions.
```

**Watch out for**
Copilot in Viva Engage needs the Microsoft 365 Copilot license (the newer one, distinct from the older premium-Engage Copilot). Copilot drafts; what to say and when to say it is your call. A public post is public, tone problems show up in the comments in real time, so the refine pass earns its keep here more than in email. For a single hard message to one person, the sensitive-email recipe is the better fit.
