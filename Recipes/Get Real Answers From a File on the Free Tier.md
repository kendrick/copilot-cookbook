**The ask**
No add-on, no problem for this one: upload the document straight into Copilot Chat and ask about it. It's one of the genuinely useful things the free tier does well.

**What you'll use**
- [Chat Over Your Files](../Surfaces/Copilot%20Chat/Chat%20Over%20Your%20Files.md) — attach a file to a Copilot Chat conversation and ask grounded questions, on Basic

**Steps**
1. Open Copilot Chat.
2. Attach the file.
3. Ask about its content, and follow up.

**Prompts**
```
Give me the key points from this PDF in five bullets.
```
```
What does this contract say about termination and notice periods?
```

**Watch out for**
This works on the free Basic tier without an add-on, though the add-on gets priority processing. It grounds on the file you upload, not your wider work data. For answers across your whole mailbox, chats, and files, you'd want the work-grounded tier instead. And don't upload anything you're not cleared to put into Copilot.
