**What it is**
A content studio inside the Microsoft 365 Copilot app for generating images, posters, video, and forms or surveys from a prompt or a template.[^1]

**Why it matters**
Design and content tasks that used to mean opening a separate tool, or filing a request with a designer, can now start from a prompt. Video runs through Clipchamp, so Create also works as an entry point into that toolset.

**Who it's for**
Anyone on Copilot Chat (Basic) or the Copilot add-on who needs quick visual or survey content without opening a dedicated design app.

**How to access**
Microsoft 365 Copilot app > Create.

**Requires**
No Copilot add-on needed for core Create. Video generation requires Clipchamp.
