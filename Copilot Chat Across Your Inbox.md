**What it is** — Outlook Copilot chat expands beyond a single thread to answer questions grounded across your whole inbox, calendar, and enterprise data.

**Why it matters** — replaces thread-by-thread digging with a single question like "what's still outstanding from this week" — the kind of cross-thread synthesis a human assistant would do.

**Who it's for** — anyone managing a heavy inbox who needs answers that span multiple threads or meetings, not just one.

**How to access** — GA as of 2026-07, as an expansion of the existing Outlook Copilot chat experience.

**Requires** — Copilot add-on for full inbox/calendar/enterprise grounding. Copilot Chat (Basic) includes standard Copilot chat in Outlook, but scoped to a single thread/web grounding rather than the full inbox-wide, work-grounded experience.
