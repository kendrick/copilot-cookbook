**What it is** — Copilot turns OneNote content into a to-do list or task set.

**Why it matters** — skips the manual step of re-reading notes to pull out action items — Copilot extracts them directly.

**Who it's for** — anyone who captures action items in OneNote and needs them as trackable tasks.

**How to access** — create-tasks option available within a OneNote page.

**Requires** — Copilot add-on license.
