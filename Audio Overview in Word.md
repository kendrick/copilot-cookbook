**What it is**
Copilot generates a real-time voice audio overview of a Word document.

**Why it matters**
It gives a listening option for reviewing a document—a first pass on a commute or a walk, before sitting down to read closely.

**Who it's for**
Anyone who wants to absorb a document without reading it on screen.

**How to access**
Not yet documented in detail beyond availability in Word's Copilot surface.

**Requires**
Microsoft 365 Copilot (add-on) license. Not included in Copilot Chat (Basic).
