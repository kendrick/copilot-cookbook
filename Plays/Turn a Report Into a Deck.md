**The ask**
The report has the substance; what it doesn't have is a narrative an executive can absorb in eight slides. Go through talking points first rather than jumping straight to slides, and the deck comes out tighter.

**What you'll use**
- [[Draft with Copilot in Word]] — turns the report into talking points first (optional, but worth the extra step)
- [[Build a Presentation With Copilot]] — builds the deck from the report or the talking points
- [[Ground a Deck in Your Work Content]] — pulls real figures and quotes instead of generic placeholders
- [[Rewrite and Summarize Slides]] — tightens the narrative once the structure is in place

**Steps**
1. In Word, turn the report into a set of talking points and a short pitch.
2. In PowerPoint, build the presentation from the report or the talking points.
3. Ground the deck in your work content so it pulls real numbers and quotes.
4. Use Rewrite and Summarize Slides to cut anything that isn't earning its place.

**Prompts**
```
Create a set of talking points from this report, then turn those into a
3-minute elevator pitch.
```
```
Create an executive presentation based on this analysis document: 8 slides,
one key takeaway per slide, exec-level language.
```

**Watch out for**
Ground a Deck in Your Work Content is new as of 2026-07; check that it's live rather than still rolling out in your tenant. In-app PowerPoint Copilot needs the Copilot add-on across the board.
