# Copilot Chat

Most of the confusion in this category traces back to one split: a free, web-grounded **Basic** tier that comes with any Microsoft 365 subscription, and a paid **add-on** tier that grounds answers in your organization's own Graph data instead—emails, files, chats, meetings.[^1] Everything else here (which model answers you, whether you can drop in a PDF, whether Copilot can see your screen) sits on top of that split.

- [[Work-Grounded Copilot Chat]] — the add-on tier: Graph-grounded, priority access.
- [[Copilot Chat (Basic)]] — the free tier: web-grounded, standard access.
- [[Chat Over Your Files]] — upload and chat over your own files, both tiers.
- [[Choose Your AI Model]] — Auto, Quick response, and Think Deeper modes.
- [[Use Claude and GPT-5.5 in Copilot]] — admin-gated access to Anthropic's Claude and OpenAI's GPT-5.5.
- [[Generate Images in Copilot Chat]] — built-in image generation.
- [[Vision in Copilot]] — screen and camera visual context, rolling out.
- [[Open Files and PDFs in Chat]] — open Office docs and PDFs directly in chat.
- [[Minor Updates — Copilot Chat]] — smaller recent tweaks.
