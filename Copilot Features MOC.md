# Copilot Features MOC

The full Microsoft 365 Copilot capability surface, organized by where it lives. Each category note links its individual feature notes. Newest capabilities (surfaced in the last ~90 days) are tagged `new: true` in their frontmatter rather than siloed off here.

Looking for "how do I solve *X*"? Start from the [[Plays MOC]] instead, which is organized by the problem rather than the app.

## In-app Copilot

- [[Word]] — draft, rewrite, chat over a document, ground on your files
- [[Excel]] — analyze data, formulas, charts, Agent Mode
- [[PowerPoint]] — build decks from a prompt or your work content, generate images
- [[Outlook]] — summarize threads, draft, coaching, chat across the inbox
- [[Teams]] — meeting recap, in-meeting Copilot, chat catch-up, calls
- [[OneNote & Loop]] — summarize notes, extract tasks, draft; collaborative pages

## The Copilot app

- [[Copilot Chat]] — work- and web-grounded chat, model modes, files, images, vision
- [[Researcher & Analyst]] — the two reasoning agents for deep research and data analysis
- [[Pages & Notebooks]] — collaborative canvases and persistent project workspaces
- [[Create]] — images, posters, video, and forms from a description
- [[Search & Work IQ]] — semantic search across your tenant and the intelligence layer under it
- [[Cowork]] — the agentic executor that plans and does multi-step work
