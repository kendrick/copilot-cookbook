**What it is** — Copilot drafts plans, ideas, lists, and rewrites directly inside OneNote.

**Why it matters** — turns a blank or rough page into a usable draft without leaving OneNote for another app.

**Who it's for** — anyone drafting structured content — plans, ideas, lists — inside OneNote.

**How to access** — draft option within a OneNote page.

**Requires** — Copilot add-on license. Rolling out on Web and Teams versions of OneNote — not yet universally GA.
