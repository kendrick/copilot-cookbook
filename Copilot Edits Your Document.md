**What it is**
Copilot now adds content directly into an existing document by default, rather than working only from a blank page. It's accessed through the Copilot icon in the left margin.

**Why it matters**
Most real writing happens inside documents that already exist. Editing in place removes the copy-paste step between a chat answer and the document itself.

**Who it's for**
Anyone extending or building out a document that's already underway.

**How to access**
Use the Copilot icon in the left margin of an open document.

**Requires**
Microsoft 365 Copilot (add-on) license. Not included in Copilot Chat (Basic).
