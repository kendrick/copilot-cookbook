**What it is**
A reasoning agent that runs deep, multi-step research across both your work content and the open web, then returns a source-cited report instead of a single chat answer. GA since June 2025.[^1]

**Why it matters**
This is the agent for "go figure this out and come back to me," not "answer this in one turn." The source citations matter for handoff: you can hand the output straight to someone else and they can check the work themselves.

**Who it's for**
Anyone doing competitive analysis, market research, or multi-source synthesis too broad for a single chat prompt. Microsoft's own use-case library shows it turning up across Sales, HR, and Marketing.[^2]

**How to access**
Microsoft 365 Copilot app > Agents > Researcher.[^1]

**Requires**
Microsoft 365 Copilot add-on license. Shares a combined pool of roughly 25 monthly queries with [Analyst](Analyst.md).

## Try it
1. Open the Microsoft 365 Copilot app.
2. Go to Agents > Researcher.
3. Ask a research question that should draw on both your organization's content and the web.

Example prompt:
> Analyze our competitors and identify industry trends that could affect our roadmap this quarter.[^2]
