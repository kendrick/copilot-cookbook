**What it is** — Copilot reviews a drafted email before you send it and flags tone, clarity, and sentiment issues.

**Why it matters** — a second read on tone before send matters most on the messages where getting it wrong is costly — sensitive updates, difficult feedback, client-facing notes.

**Who it's for** — anyone sending emails where tone carries risk, including HR and client-facing roles.

**How to access** — coaching option available on a drafted email in Outlook, before sending.

**Requires** — Copilot add-on license.
