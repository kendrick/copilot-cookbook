**What it is** — Copilot generates a "Summary by Copilot" of a long email thread, cited back to the source messages.

**Why it matters** — reconstructing a sprawling thread by scrolling is the single biggest time sink in inbox triage; a cited summary gets you to the point in seconds.

**Who it's for** — anyone managing high-volume or long-running email threads.

**How to access** — Summary by Copilot appears on eligible email threads in Outlook.

**Requires** — Copilot add-on license.
