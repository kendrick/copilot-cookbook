**What it is** — Copilot summarizes OneNote notes, including audio transcriptions and handwritten content, with citations.

**Why it matters** — freeform notes only pay off if you can retrieve them fast; a cited summary works whether the source was typed, recorded, or handwritten.

**Who it's for** — anyone using OneNote for meeting notes, working notes, or voice-recorded input.

**How to access** — summarize option available within a OneNote page.

**Requires** — Copilot add-on license. Not available on notebooks or sections protected by Windows Information Protection (WIP).
