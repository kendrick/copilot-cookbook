**What it is**
A Viva Insights dashboard purpose-built for Copilot, showing adoption across both M365 Copilot and Copilot Chat, plus readiness recommendations for where to focus rollout effort next.

**Why it matters**
"Is anyone actually using this" is the question that determines whether a Copilot rollout gets renewed or quietly shelved. This is where that answer lives, broken down by surface rather than as one undifferentiated number.

**Who it's for**
Admins and program sponsors tracking rollout health; useful evidence for a rollout business case.

**How to access**
Viva Insights > Copilot dashboard.

**Requires**
Admin configuration; Viva Insights access.
