**What it is**
Smaller governance tweaks that don't warrant their own note but are worth knowing about.

- Files that Copilot generates now inherit the highest sensitivity label among their source documents, GA as of July 2026.[^1]
- Agent metrics and insights are rolling out for custom reporting on agent usage: currently Preview, targeting GA later in 2026.[^2]
- Org messages (the admin-to-user notification tool) now support email delivery and finer-grained targeting.[^2]
- AI-generated content is getting watermarking support to flag it as machine-produced.[^2]
