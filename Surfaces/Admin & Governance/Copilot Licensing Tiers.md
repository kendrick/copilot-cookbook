**What it is**
Three ways to get Copilot, in increasing order of capability:

1. **Copilot Chat (Basic)**: free with M365. Web-grounded only, standard access, Copilot chat available in Outlook and Teams. No in-app Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, or OneNote. Agents run pay-as-you-go only.
2. **Copilot Chat, pay-as-you-go**: adds metered, work-grounded agents on org data. Requires a Copilot Studio license, and metered agents are off by default until an admin enables them.
3. **Microsoft 365 Copilot add-on**: paid per user. Work-grounded via Microsoft Graph, priority access, full in-app Copilot everywhere, Researcher & Analyst, and full custom/autonomous agent capability.

**Why it matters**
Questions about "why can't I do X in Copilot" almost always trace back to a tier gap, not a bug. Knowing the three tiers means you can diagnose the gap on the spot instead of taking it as a follow-up.

**Who it's for**
Anyone advising on rollout scope, budget, or troubleshooting a feature gap.

**How to access**
M365 admin center > Billing > Licenses, or Copilot Control System for per-group assignment.

**Requires**
Admin configuration to assign and manage licenses.
