**What it is**
Microsoft's standing commitment that prompts and responses in Copilot are not used to train the underlying foundation models. It applies across all clouds, and commercial data stays isolated from the model providers' own training pipelines.

**Why it matters**
This is the single fact that unblocks most security and compliance hesitation about Copilot. "Does my confidential prompt end up training someone else's model" is usually the first question in the room, and the answer is no, by design, not just by policy promise.

**Who it's for**
Anyone briefing security, legal, or management stakeholders on Copilot risk.

**How to access**
No action needed. It's a standing platform guarantee, not a toggle.

**Requires**
Admin configuration. Applies automatically under any Copilot license tier.
