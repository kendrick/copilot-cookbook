**What it is**
The Microsoft 365 Copilot app for iOS and Android: hands-free voice chat, summaries of email, meetings, and chats, and quick drafting, all tied to your work account.[^1]

**Why it matters**
The moments when catching up matters most, right before a meeting, on the way somewhere, are exactly the moments a laptop isn't open. Voice-first on the phone fits how you actually work when you're away from the desk.

**Who it's for**
Anyone who spends real parts of the day away from a screen: field roles, frequent travelers, back-to-back meeting days.

**How to access**
Install from the App Store or Google Play, sign in with your work or school account. Voice conversations are enabled by default.

**Requires**
A Microsoft 365 work or school account for chat; the Microsoft 365 Copilot add-on to ground answers in your work data. This is the enterprise Copilot app, distinct from the consumer Microsoft Copilot app.
