**What it is** — Post-meeting recap and Q&A. Copilot summarizes what happened, and Intelligent Recap layers on AI-generated highlights, chapters, and speaker notes. Meeting chat also lets you catch up on discussion after the fact.

**Why it matters** — turns "I missed that meeting" from a scramble to rewatch the recording into a few minutes of reading a cited recap.

**Who it's for** — anyone who missed a meeting, or wants a fast recap instead of reviewing the full recording.

**How to access** — recap surfaces in the meeting chat/recap tab once the meeting ends.

**Requires** — Meeting transcription must be turned ON. Standard recap needs the Copilot add-on plus transcription; Intelligent Recap specifically requires Teams Premium OR a Copilot license.

## Try it

1. Open the meeting chat or recap tab after a transcribed meeting ends.
2. Ask Copilot a question about the discussion.

> Example prompt: "Where do we disagree on this topic?"

> Example prompt: "What ideas were discussed?"
