**What it is** — Real-time Copilot assistance inside a live Teams meeting, grounded in the transcript as it's generated.

**Why it matters** — joining a meeting late no longer means whispering "what did I miss" to a colleague — you can ask Copilot directly, mid-meeting.

**Who it's for** — meeting attendees and hosts who need live context without interrupting the discussion.

**How to access** — Copilot panel inside an active Teams meeting.

**Requires** — Copilot add-on license; a live meeting transcript.
