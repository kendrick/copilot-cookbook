**What it is** — An AI agent that takes real-time collaborative notes during a scheduled Teams meeting, tracks the agenda with timers, and captures decisions and action items as the discussion happens. Action items can sync to Planner.

**Why it matters** — Note-taking usually falls to whoever's least busy and gets done worst. Facilitator keeps a shared, running record nobody has to babysit, so the group can actually talk instead of transcribing.

**Who it's for** — Organizers and participants in scheduled Teams meetings who want the notes and follow-ups handled live.

**How to access** — When scheduling: meeting Options > Copilot and other AI > turn Facilitator on. Or in the meeting: More actions > Turn on Facilitator.

**Requires** — Microsoft 365 Copilot license. Scheduled meetings only, not channel meetings, instant meetings, or calls. It can't read sensitivity-labeled content. Still in preview.
