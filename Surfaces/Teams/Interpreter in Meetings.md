**What it is** — Real-time speech-to-speech interpretation in a Teams meeting. Each participant hears the meeting in their preferred language, in a simulated voice that echoes the speaker's, for meetings up to 1,000 people.

**Why it matters** — A multilingual meeting no longer needs a human interpreter or a translated recap after the fact. Everyone participates live, in the language they think in.

**Who it's for** — Cross-language teams, global functions, and any meeting where language would otherwise split the room.

**How to access** — More actions > Language and speech > Interpreter > Turn on for me, then choose a language. On mobile: More > Turn on Interpreter.

**Requires** — Microsoft 365 Copilot license, with 20 hours per person per month included; the organizer needs a Copilot or Teams Premium license. Supports a fixed set of languages (including English, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Italian, German, French, and Korean). Not available in ad-hoc, Teams Free, town hall, or webinar meetings.
