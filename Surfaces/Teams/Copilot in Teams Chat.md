**What it is** — Copilot chat scoped to a single Teams chat thread, with lookback of up to 30 days and cited responses.

**Why it matters** — answers "what happened in this chat" without scrolling back through weeks of messages.

**Who it's for** — anyone in an active, longer-running Teams chat thread.

**How to access** — Copilot chat option inside a Teams chat thread.

**Requires** — Copilot add-on license for full functionality. Also available on Copilot Chat (Basic), which includes standard Copilot chat in Teams — though scope and priority access differ from the add-on tier.
