**What it is** — Copilot support on Teams VoIP and PSTN calls, surfacing key points, owners, and next steps.

**Why it matters** — captures action items from phone-style calls the same way it does for scheduled meetings, so 1:1s and customer calls don't fall through the cracks.

**Who it's for** — anyone taking calls through Teams Calling — customer calls, 1:1s.

**How to access** — available during and after a Teams VoIP or PSTN call.

**Requires** — Copilot add-on license.
