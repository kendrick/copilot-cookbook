**What it is**
A contextual AI assistant inside Viva Engage that helps draft and refine posts, announcements, and storylines, discover conversations, and connect information across the org. It works across communities, storylines, campaigns, the home feed, and single conversations, and stays inside each user's existing access.[^1]

**Why it matters**
A company-wide message lives or dies on tone. Drafting it inside Engage means the phrasing gets a pass before it reaches the whole organization, instead of after, when the reply thread has already turned.

**Who it's for**
Internal comms, HR, and leaders who broadcast to the organization rather than to a single inbox.

**How to access**
The Copilot icon at the bottom-left of the Viva Engage home page, or the search bar, once an admin has assigned the Microsoft 365 Copilot license.[^2]

**Requires**
A Microsoft 365 Copilot license. This is distinct from the earlier Copilot in Viva Engage that required a premium Viva Engage license.
