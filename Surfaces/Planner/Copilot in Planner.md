**What it is**
Chat-based planning inside the Planner app in Teams. Describe the work and Copilot generates a full plan of tasks, subtasks, buckets, and goals; it can also add individual items to an existing plan and answer questions about where the plan stands.[^1]

**Why it matters**
The gap between "we agreed on the work" and "the work is a tracked plan" is where good intentions die. Generating the structure from a description closes that gap in one step instead of a manual afternoon in Planner.

**Who it's for**
Project managers and team leads who run their work through Planner in Teams.

**How to access**
Teams > Planner > open or create a premium plan > the Copilot icon in the plan's top menu.[^2]

**Requires**
A premium Planner plan (Planner and Project Plan 3 or 5) or a Microsoft 365 Copilot license. Still in preview, with a 30-day trial available.

## Try it
1. Open a premium plan in the Planner app in Teams and select Copilot.
2. Describe the project, or use a suggested prompt.

> Example prompt: Generate a plan to launch the Q4 newsletter, with tasks, buckets, and a goal for each phase.
