**What it is**
Sort, filter, and conditionally format data using natural language, and add, rename, or delete worksheets.

**Why it matters**
Routine cleanup work—the kind that eats time without needing real judgment—moves from manual clicks to a single request.

**Who it's for**
Anyone maintaining a working spreadsheet.

**How to access**
Copilot pane in Excel.

**Requires**
Microsoft 365 Copilot (add-on) license. Not included in Copilot Chat (Basic).

## Try it
1. Open the Copilot pane in Excel.
2. Describe the sort, filter, or formatting rule you want applied.

> Example prompt: Apply conditional formatting to highlight values over 1000
