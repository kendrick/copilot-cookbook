**What it is**
Copilot generates data and formulas from a plain-English request.

**Why it matters**
It cuts the time spent looking up formula syntax, especially for functions nobody has memorized.

**Who it's for**
Anyone building or extending a spreadsheet who'd rather describe what they need than hand-write it.

**How to access**
Copilot icon lower-right in Excel.

**Requires**
Microsoft 365 Copilot (add-on) license. Not included in Copilot Chat (Basic).

## Try it
1. Open the Copilot pane (icon lower-right in Excel).
2. Describe the calculation you want.

> Example prompt: Sum revenue by quarter and add a trend analysis
