**What it is**
A chat-only mode that identifies insights, trends, and outliers in a worksheet without changing the underlying data.

**Why it matters**
Non-destructive analysis means anyone can interrogate a spreadsheet without risking the source data—no separate copy needed just to ask a question.

**Who it's for**
Analysts, managers, and anyone reviewing a dataset before acting on it.

**How to access**
Open the Copilot pane in Excel (icon lower-right) and ask questions about the selected data.

**Requires**
Microsoft 365 Copilot (add-on) license. Not included in Copilot Chat (Basic).

## Try it
1. Open the Copilot pane in Excel (icon lower-right) with your data selected.
2. Ask a question about what the data shows.

> Example prompt: What products have declining sales?
