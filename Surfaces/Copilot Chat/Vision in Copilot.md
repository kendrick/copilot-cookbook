**What it is**
Copilot Chat can take in visual context from your screen or a camera, not just typed and uploaded text, and describe or reason about what's on screen or in view. Rolling out since July 2026.[^1]

**Why it matters**
This closes a real gap for people who don't want to type a description first: point a camera at a whiteboard, or let Copilot see what's already open. Worth flagging as not yet universal—rollout means some users get it well before others.

**Who it's for**
Copilot Chat users on devices with a supported camera, once rollout reaches their tenant.

**How to access**
Rolling out to Copilot Chat; availability depends on tenant and device rollout stage.[^1]

**Requires**
Copilot Chat access. Still rolling out, so not yet available to every tenant.
