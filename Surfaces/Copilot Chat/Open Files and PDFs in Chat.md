**What it is**
Open Office documents and PDFs directly inside Copilot Chat instead of uploading them as a detached attachment. GA as of July 2026.[^1]

**Why it matters**
The earlier upload flow ([[Chat Over Your Files]]) treats a file as something you attach and ask about. This treats it as something you open and work inside, closer to how people actually think about a document. Small distinction, real difference in how natural the conversation feels.

**Who it's for**
Anyone working from a PDF or Office file who wants to stay inside the chat rather than switching apps.

**How to access**
Open a supported Office document or PDF directly within Copilot Chat.[^1]

**Requires**
Copilot Chat access, either tier.
