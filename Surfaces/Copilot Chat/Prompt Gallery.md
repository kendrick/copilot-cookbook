**What it is**
A built-in library of ready-made prompts you can browse, run, save, and share, organized by app and by job. You can also save your own prompts and share them with a team.[^1]

**Why it matters**
The blank Copilot box is the real adoption wall: people freeze on "what do I even ask it." The gallery is the fastest way past that, and saved prompts let a champion standardize a team on the handful that actually work instead of everyone reinventing them.

**Who it's for**
New users looking for a starting point, and champions seeding a team's first prompts.

**How to access**
The prompt gallery in Copilot Chat and the Microsoft 365 Copilot app; also published at the Microsoft adoption site.[^2]

**Requires**
Copilot Chat (Basic) or the Microsoft 365 Copilot add-on.
