**What it is**
Copilot Chat can now answer using frontier models beyond Microsoft's own: Anthropic's Claude (GA June 2026) and OpenAI's GPT-5.5 in two variants, GPT-5.5 Instant and GPT-5.5 Thinking (GA May 2026). Admins turn access on per user or group, so it isn't on by default.[^1][^2]

**Why it matters**
This is often the headline: Copilot Chat is no longer a single-model product. What a given person can get out of it now depends on what their admin switched on, not just which license they hold, so check that before advising someone on a model behavior they may not even have access to.

**Who it's for**
Copilot add-on users whose admin has enabled a given model for their account or group.

**How to access**
Enabled by admins in the Copilot Control System, per user or group. Once enabled, the model appears as a selectable option in Copilot Chat.[^1]

**Requires**
Microsoft 365 Copilot add-on license, plus admin opt-in for the specific model.
