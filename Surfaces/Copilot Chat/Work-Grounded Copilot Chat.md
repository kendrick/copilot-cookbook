**What it is**
The paid tier of Copilot Chat. Answers are grounded in your organization's Microsoft Graph data—emails, Teams chats, meetings, SharePoint and OneDrive files—instead of just the open web, and requests run at priority capacity rather than shared, standard capacity.[^1]

**Why it matters**
This is the difference between Copilot telling you what's true on the internet and Copilot telling you what's true inside your company. Work grounding is what makes a prompt like "what did finance decide in yesterday's meeting" answerable at all.

**Who it's for**
Anyone licensed on the Microsoft 365 Copilot add-on. This is the default entry point for most named users on a paid seat.

**How to access**
Available wherever Copilot Chat appears for add-on-licensed users: the Microsoft 365 Copilot app, Teams, Outlook, and the in-app chat pane across Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and OneNote.[^1]

**Requires**
Microsoft 365 Copilot add-on license.

## Try it
1. Open Copilot Chat from any Microsoft 365 app or the Copilot app.
2. Ask a question that spans multiple sources of work content—email, chat, files.

Example prompt:
> Tell me what's new about [topic] organized by emails, chats, and files.[^2]
