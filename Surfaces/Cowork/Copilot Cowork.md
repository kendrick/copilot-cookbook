**What it is**
An agentic executor that plans, executes, and delivers multi-step work, pausing for your approval at each step. Supports up to 50 custom skills and plugins.[^1]

**Why it matters**
Most Copilot experiences respond to a single prompt. Cowork takes on a task end to end, breaking it into steps, doing the work, and checking in for approval along the way, closer to delegating to a capable team member than to querying a chatbot. It reached general availability on June 16, 2026, following a Frontier preview that started in March.[^2]

**Who it's for**
Teams with recurring multi-step work, such as status reporting or research compilation, who want to hand off execution rather than drive it prompt by prompt.

**How to access**
An admin has to enable Cowork first; it's off by default.

**Requires**
Copilot add-on (USL) + usage-based billing through Copilot Credits + admin enablement.
