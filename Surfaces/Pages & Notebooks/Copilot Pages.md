**What it is**
A persistent, collaborative canvas that Copilot builds from a chat response, so a useful answer stops being disposable chat history.[^1]

**Why it matters**
A good chat response scrolls out of view the moment the conversation moves on. Pages gives you a save point: open a response as a document, keep editing it, and share it with others, without leaving the flow of chat and without needing a Copilot license to do any of that.

**Who it's for**
Anyone in the org with Entra ID and SharePoint/OneDrive access. No Copilot add-on is required, so it's available even on the free Copilot Chat (Basic) tier.

**How to access**
Select the pencil icon below a chat response to open it as a Page.

**Requires**
Entra ID + SharePoint/OneDrive. No Copilot add-on license needed.
