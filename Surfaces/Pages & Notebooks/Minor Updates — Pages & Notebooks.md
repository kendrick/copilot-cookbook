Small enhancements shipping alongside the major Pages & Notebooks features, rolling out through July 2026.

- **Mind maps**: Notebooks can generate a visual mind map from its sources, a structural view of a project alongside the chat and reference panes.[^1]
- **OneNote mobile support**: Notebooks are now accessible from OneNote's mobile apps, not just desktop and web.[^1]
- **Multimodal capture**: capture photos, sketches, or voice notes directly into a notebook from mobile.[^1]
- **Outlook email references**: pull specific emails into a notebook as reference sources, alongside files and meeting notes.[^1]
