**What it is**
AI authoring inside SharePoint: create sites, pages, lists, libraries, and interactive reports from a natural-language description. It includes template-based page creation, Design Ideas for layout suggestions, and rich-text rewriting to adjust tone or length.[^1]

**Why it matters**
Standing up a decent SharePoint page is mostly layout fiddling, and it's the reason a lot of team sites never get built. Describing the page you want and letting Copilot draft the structure removes the part people avoid.

**Who it's for**
Site owners, internal comms, and anyone who publishes to a team or communication site.

**How to access**
Enabled by default for licensed users once the preview reaches the tenant; the authoring tools appear in the page and site editing experience.[^2]

**Requires**
Microsoft 365 Copilot license, at no extra cost during preview. Daily and weekly usage limits apply while in preview. Not available in Government (GCC, GCC High, DoD) or air-gapped clouds, and the Restricted Content Discovery setting disables it wherever it's turned on.

## Try it
1. Start a new page and pick a template (Newsletter, Event, Status Update, Process), or describe the page you want.
2. Fill the prompted fields, then use Design Ideas for a layout.
3. Use the rich-text rewrite to adjust tone or length, and preview before publishing.
