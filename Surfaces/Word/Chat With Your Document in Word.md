**What it is**
A chat pane inside Word that answers questions, summarizes, and cites its sources from the open document.

**Why it matters**
A long document is easier to interrogate than to reread. A reviewer can ask what changed in section three instead of hunting for it.

**Who it's for**
Reviewers, editors, and anyone catching up on a document they didn't write.

**How to access**
Open the Copilot chat pane while a document is open.

**Requires**
Microsoft 365 Copilot (add-on) license. Not included in Copilot Chat (Basic).

## Try it
1. Open the Copilot chat pane with the document open.
2. Ask a question about the document's content.

> Example prompt: What are the risks mentioned in the document?
