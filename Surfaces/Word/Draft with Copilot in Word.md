**What it is**
Copilot drafts new content directly in a blank document from a prompt typed into the Copilot box at the top of the page.

**Why it matters**
It replaces the blank-page problem. A first draft exists in seconds, giving the writer something to react to and edit instead of a cursor blinking on an empty page.

**Who it's for**
Anyone starting a document from scratch—briefs, memos, proposals, reports.

**How to access**
Open a new or existing Word document and use the Copilot box at the top of the page.

**Requires**
Microsoft 365 Copilot (add-on) license. Not included in Copilot Chat (Basic).

## Try it
1. Open a new Word document.
2. In the Copilot box at the top of the page, describe the document you want.

> Example prompt: Write a job offer letter for a sales position at Contoso. The start date is August 1st, and the salary is $60,000 per year plus bonuses
