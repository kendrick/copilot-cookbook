**What it is**
Copilot adds slides and images to an existing presentation from a prompt.

**Why it matters**
Extending a deck stays inside PowerPoint instead of round-tripping through chat and copy-paste.

**Who it's for**
Anyone building out a deck slide by slide.

**How to access**
Copilot pane in PowerPoint.

**Requires**
Microsoft 365 Copilot (add-on) license. Not included in Copilot Chat (Basic).

## Try it
1. Open the Copilot pane in PowerPoint.
2. Describe the slide you want added.

> Example prompt: Add a slide about competitive differentiation
