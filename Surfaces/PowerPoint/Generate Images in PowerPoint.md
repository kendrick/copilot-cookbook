**What it is**
Copilot generates original images for a slide as part of adding content to a deck, now including newer image models (FLUX.2, MAI).

**Why it matters**
Custom visuals without leaving PowerPoint or hunting for stock art that almost fits.

**Who it's for**
Anyone building a deck that needs original imagery rather than stock photos.

**How to access**
Copilot pane in PowerPoint, image generation option.

**Requires**
Microsoft 365 Copilot (add-on) license. Not included in Copilot Chat (Basic).
