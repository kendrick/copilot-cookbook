**What it is**
Copilot rewrites slide text and summarizes an entire presentation.

**Why it matters**
Tightening slide copy and producing a quick summary for someone who missed the meeting both become a single request instead of a manual pass through every slide.

**Who it's for**
Anyone editing an existing deck or briefing someone from one.

**How to access**
Copilot pane in PowerPoint.

**Requires**
Microsoft 365 Copilot (add-on) license. Not included in Copilot Chat (Basic).

## Try it
1. Open the Copilot pane in PowerPoint.
2. Ask Copilot to rewrite a slide's text or summarize the deck.

> Example prompt: Summarize the main points of this presentation
